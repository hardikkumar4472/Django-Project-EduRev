How Room Exchange Works at LPU

From preference matching to key handover, every step is protected by mathematical atomicity and official authorization.

1

Submit Request

Specify your preferred hostels (BH-1 to BH-10 or GH-1 to GH-10), room occupancy types, and optional lifestyle preferences.

2

Discover Swaps

Our graph algorithm automatically finds direct 2-way swaps and multi-party cyclic chains with 100% consensus.

3

Warden Approval

Hostel Wardens and Chief Warden inspect eligibility, discipline records, and give cryptographic approval in one click.

4

Move & Scan QR

Print your official transfer certificate, present the digital QR to the caretaker at the key desk, and move into your new room.

Hostel Administration Policies

Official Guidelines & Eligibility

  • ✓ Gender Segregation Enforced: Male students can only exchange rooms within Boys Hostels (BH-1 to BH-10); female students within Girls Hostels (GH-1 to GH-10).
  • ✓ One Active Exchange Request: Students may maintain only one active exchange request at any given time to guarantee fair cycle matching.
  • ✓ All-or-Nothing Consensus: Multi-party rotations require 100% mutual acceptance before proceeding to Warden authorization.
  • ✓ Atomic Lock Protection: Database row-level locks prevent race conditions and partial transfers during concurrent approvals.
