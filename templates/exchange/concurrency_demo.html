Simulates real-world race conditions when multiple wardens approve overlapping multi-party chains simultaneously.

Invariant: Zero Partial Transfers

The Problem: ABBA Deadlocks & Race Conditions

Suppose Warden 1 approves Chain 1 affecting [Bed 203, Bed 304] while Warden 2 approves Chain 2 affecting [Bed 304, Bed 203]. If locks are acquired haphazardly, Transaction 1 locks Bed 203 and waits for Bed 304, while Transaction 2 locks Bed 304 and waits for Bed 203. This is a classic ABBA Deadlock.

The Solution: Primary-Key Ordered Locking

In occupancy_service.py, we collect all bed IDs and strictly sort them ascending by primary key before calling Bed.objects.select_for_update().filter(id__in=sorted_ids). Both transactions attempt to lock the lower ID first, completely eliminating cyclic deadlock wait graphs!
