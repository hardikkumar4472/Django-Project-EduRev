Campus Safety & Discipline

Mandatory Living Policies

Compliance with university housing policies is mandatory for all hostel residents. Violations result in disciplinary demerit points and suspension of room exchange privileges.

🕒

Biometric Curfew & Movement

Biometric fingerprint attendance is recorded nightly at hostel entrance turnstiles.
• Girls Hostels (GH-1 to GH-10): 07:30 PM sharp.
• Boys Hostels (BH-1 to BH-10): 09:30 PM sharp.
Late library or laboratory access passes must be applied for and approved by the Hostel Warden via the university portal prior to 05:00 PM.

🚫

Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy

In compliance with Supreme Court orders and UGC Regulations, Lovely Professional University maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form.
Any student found guilty faces immediate expulsion, forfeiture of hostel allocation, and registration of a criminal FIR with state police authorities.

🔇

Mandatory Silence Hours

Quiet study and resting hours are enforced daily between 10:30 PM and 06:00 AM.
Playing musical instruments, television speakers, loud hallway conversations, or disorderly gatherings during these hours is strictly prohibited to protect the academic study environment.

⚡

Permitted & Prohibited Electricals

• Permitted: Laptops, tablets, mobile chargers, study desk lamps.
• Strictly Prohibited: Immersion heating rods, electric kettles, induction stoves, room heaters, and electric irons.
Unauthorized heating devices cause fire risks and will be confiscated with a monetary penalty.

🍽️

Central Mess & Hygiene Standards

Residents must take all meals in the central dining hall. Carrying food, utensils, or glassware to student rooms is prohibited to prevent pest infestations.
Mess Timings: Breakfast (07:30–09:30 AM), Lunch (12:00–02:00 PM), Tea (05:00–06:00 PM), Dinner (07:30–09:45 PM).

🧳

Room Shifting & Inventory Protocol

Approved room swap passes are valid for 72 hours from the Warden's digital signature.
Before receiving new keys, the resident must complete an inventory inspection (bed cot, mattress, study desk, almirah, fan, lights) with the Block Caretaker and surrender the old room keys.

Division of Student Welfare (DSW) Oversight

All hostel allocations, cyclic swaps, disciplinary evaluations, and caretaker inspections are audited by the Directorate of Student Welfare under ISO-9001 quality management guidelines. For policy inquiries or special disability accommodations, contact the DSW Central Helpdesk in Block 32.

Compliance FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions on Conduct & Rules

Essential answers regarding hostel discipline, room cleanliness, and damage responsibilities.

❓ What happens if a resident fails to shift rooms within the 72-hour pass window? ▾

If the key handover and Caretaker inspection are not concluded within 72 hours of approval, the digital pass expires automatically to avoid holding beds in limbo. The allocation reverts to the original status, and the applicant must submit a new request.

❓ Who pays for damage found in the room during Caretaker inspection? ▾

The outgoing resident is solely accountable for the condition of fixtures and furniture allocated to them upon check-in. Any deliberate damage or broken inventory will be assessed by the Estate Maintenance Division and debited from the student's security deposit.

❓ Are day-scholar students permitted inside hostel buildings? ▾

No. Day scholars and non-resident students are strictly barred from entering residential hostel blocks at all times. Violations lead to immediate disciplinary hearings before the Proctorial Board.

❓ Can I keep a refrigerator or personal microwave in the room? ▾

Personal refrigerators and microwaves are not permitted inside individual rooms due to circuit load constraints. Common refrigerators are provided in designated dining or pantry areas across selected blocks.
