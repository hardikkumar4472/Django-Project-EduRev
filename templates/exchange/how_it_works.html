The 4-Stage Digital Lifecycle

Every room exchange follows a rigorous, tamper-proof four-stage state machine from initial submission to key collection.

1

Submit Room Preferences

Log in with your University Registration Number. Verify your current allocated room and select your target hostels (BH-1 to BH-10 for boys, GH-1 to GH-10 for girls), preferred floors, and AC / Non-AC preferences.

⚡ Instant Eligibility Validation
2

Cycle Discovery & Matching

Our graph algorithm runs continuously. It identifies both direct pairwise swaps (A ↔ B) and multi-hop circular rotations (A → B → C → A) to break deadlocks and maximize opportunities.

🔄 Multi-Party Circular Matching
3

Unanimous Consensus & Warden Review

All students in a matched cycle must review and unanimously accept the proposed rooms. Once approved by students, the request is digitally forwarded to the respective Hostel Wardens for discipline verification.

🛡️ Double-Gated Authority Clearance
4

QR Transfer Pass & Key Handover

Upon Warden sign-off, download your official Transfer Letter with an encrypted QR code. Present it to your Block Caretaker desk within 72 hours for inventory clearance and immediate key handover.

🔑 Digital QR Caretaker Sign-off
Deterministic Cyclic Graph Engine

Solving Swap Deadlocks with 3-Way & 4-Way Rotations

In traditional paper-based hostel systems, students can only swap if they find someone who wants their exact room and vice versa. LPU's algorithm models the entire campus as a Directed Graph, discovering circular permutations where:

  • Student A (in BH-1) moves to Student B's room in BH-3
  • Student B (in BH-3) moves to Student C's room in BH-6
  • Student C (in BH-6) moves to Student A's room in BH-1

All three allocations execute simultaneously in a single atomic database transaction. If any student cancels, all beds are safely reverted without locking students out.

Moves to BH-1 (Cycle Complete!)
✅ 100% Deterministic • Zero Partial Transfers

Step-by-Step Guidance

Questions About the Process

Clear answers to practical questions regarding requests, timelines, and approvals.

❓ Can I edit my preferences after submitting a room exchange request? ▾

Yes. If your request is still in 'ACTIVE' status and has not yet been matched into a binding proposal, you can withdraw your request with one click from your Student Cockpit and submit an updated set of target hostels or floor preferences.

❓ How long do students have to accept or decline a discovered swap proposal? ▾

Students have a 48-hour response window to review the matched room details and accept the proposal. If any participant does not respond within the timeframe, the proposal expires automatically and beds are unlocked.

❓ What happens if my swap involves shifting between AC and Non-AC rooms? ▾

The system calculates the exact pro-rated difference in semester hostel fees based on the university tariff table. Once the transfer is completed at the Caretaker desk, the Accounts Division automatically updates your University Management System (UMS) fee account.

❓ Do I need to visit the Warden's office in person? ▾

No. The entire review and approval process is 100% digital. Wardens review academic, disciplinary, and fee clearance records through their administrative portal. You will receive an SMS and email notification once your pass is signed.
