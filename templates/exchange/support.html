Grievance Redressal Ladder

Follow our formal university resolution hierarchy for swift and auditable handling of any issue.

Level 1

Block Caretaker Desk

Electrical breakdowns, tap leaks, furniture issues, key replacement, physical room inspection during shifts.

Turnaround: 4–12 Hours
Level 2

Hostel Warden In-Charge

Room exchange approvals, roommate disputes, night passes, attendance irregularities, mess feedback.

Turnaround: 24–48 Hours
Level 3

Chief Warden Secretariat

Cross-hostel transfers, medical priority requests, safety escalations, disciplinary reviews.

Turnaround: 48–72 Hours
Level 4

Dean (DSW) Directorate

Final appellate authority for administrative policy exceptions and central housing governance.

Turnaround: 3–5 Business Days

Helpdesk FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions on Support & Grievances

❓ What is the official procedure for filing a hostel maintenance ticket? ▾

Residents can log repair tickets 24/7 through the LPU Touch mobile app or visit their ground floor Caretaker desk. Routine plumbing, electrical, and carpentry issues are resolved within 4 to 12 hours under university SLAs.

❓ How do I request emergency medical aid during night hours? ▾

Immediately contact your floor Caretaker or call the Uni-Hospital Emergency Line directly at +91 1824 517108 / Ext. 108. The campus ambulance will be dispatched to your hostel entrance within 3 to 5 minutes.

❓ Who handles fee refund or billing disputes related to room category changes? ▾

Fee adjustments are governed by the University Accounts Division in Sector 32. Any debit or credit discrepancies after room shifting can be escalated via ticket to accounts@lpu.co.in or visited at Window 4 in Block 32.
