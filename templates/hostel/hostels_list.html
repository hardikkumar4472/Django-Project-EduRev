Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About LPU Hostels

Find answers to common questions regarding room allocations, facilities, shifting policies, and guest rules.

❓ How are room seaters and categories allocated in LPU Hostels? ▾

Rooms are offered in 1-Seater (Single), 2-Seater (Double), 3-Seater (Triple), 4-Seater (Quad), and 7/8-Seater Dormitory layouts, with both AC and Non-AC configurations. Allotments are managed digitally based on applicant preference, program seniority, and availability at the start of each academic session.

❓ Are campus facilities such as Gym, Laundry, and Wi-Fi included in the hostel fee? ▾

Yes. High-speed campus Wi-Fi, electricity, 24/7 power backup, central reading lounges, and water purification are fully included in the standard hostel fee. The gymnasium and automated laundry tokens are provided as complimentary residential services governed by DSW policies.

❓ Can I request a room transfer between different hostel buildings (e.g., from BH-2 to BH-8)? ▾

Yes! Our automated exchange engine allows you to select any of your preferred hostels as target destinations. When a matching direct or multi-party rotation is found, the system coordinates approval across both Wardens and enables your transfer seamlessly.

❓ What basic furniture and amenities are provided inside the room? ▾

Every resident is provided an individual steel cot bed with mattress, a designated study desk with ergonomic chair, a spacious lockable wardrobe/almirah, book rack, and individual electrical sockets. AC rooms feature wall-mounted split air conditioning units with automated thermostatic control.

❓ What is the process for lodging maintenance requests (electrical, plumbing, carpentry)? ▾

Residents can log maintenance tickets 24/7 through the LPU Touch mobile app or at the Caretaker desk on the ground floor of each block. Routine repairs (e.g. tubelight replacement, tap leakage) are resolved within 4 to 12 hours under university service-level agreements.

❓ What are the regulations regarding visitors and parents? ▾

Parents and verified guardians may visit students in designated hostel visitor lounges between 09:00 AM and 06:00 PM on weekends and university holidays after submitting government ID proof at the main security reception. Overnight stays inside student rooms are strictly prohibited.
