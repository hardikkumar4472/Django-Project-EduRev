Step-by-Step Transparency

How Room Exchange Works at LPU

Every room swap is governed by strict deterministic consistency, consensus protocols, and digital approvals.

1

Submit Preferences

Specify your current room, preferred LPU hostels (BH-1 to BH-10 for male students, GH-1 to GH-10 for female students), floor, and room sharing types.

2

Graph Swap Matching

The cycle discovery engine searches for direct 2-way swaps and multi-hop rotations (A → B → C → A) to maximize swap opportunities.

3

Consensus & Approval

All participating students accept the proposal unanimously. The Hostel Warden and Chief Warden review discipline records and digitally approve the pass.

4

Move & Key Handover

Download your certified transfer pass with an encrypted QR code. Present it to the caretaker desk to verify room inspection and collect your new keys.

Deterministic Cyclic Graph Matching

Solving Deadlocks with Multi-Hop Swaps

When two students cannot swap directly, our algorithm connects a 3-way or 4-way circular chain. All participants move simultaneously with database row-level locking, ensuring zero partial transfers.

Institutional Policy

Official Guidelines & Code of Conduct

✓
Gender Segregation Enforced: Male students can only exchange rooms within Boys Hostels (BH-1 to BH-10); female students within Girls Hostels (GH-1 to GH-10).
✓
One Active Request at a Time: Students may maintain only one active request to prevent queue starvation and ensure swift cycle settlements.
✓
Mandatory Physical Inspection: Caretaker verification is required before electronic key handover is recorded in the central database.
