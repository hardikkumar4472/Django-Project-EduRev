How Room Exchange Works at LPU

Every room swap is governed by strict deterministic consistency, consensus protocols, and digital approvals.

1

Submit Preferences

Specify your current room, preferred LPU hostels (BH-1 to BH-10 for male students, GH-1 to GH-10 for female students), floor, and room sharing types.

2

Graph Swap Matching

The cycle discovery engine searches for direct 2-way swaps and multi-hop rotations (A → B → C → A) to maximize swap opportunities.

3

Consensus & Approval

All participating students accept the proposal unanimously. The Hostel Warden and Chief Warden review discipline records and digitally approve the pass.

4

Move & Key Handover

Download your certified transfer pass with an encrypted QR code. Present it to the caretaker desk to verify room inspection and collect your new keys.

Institutional Policy & Code of Living

LPU Residential Regulations & Conduct Rules

Living in Lovely Professional University residences entails adherence to safety, mutual respect, and university housing governance. Review the mandatory policies below before initiating a room exchange.

🕒

Biometric Curfew & Movement

Strict biometric punch-in is enforced across all residences. Girls Hostels (GH-1 to GH-10) observe curfew at 07:30 PM; Boys Hostels (BH-1 to BH-10) at 09:30 PM. Late night library or laboratory passes must be approved through the DSW portal in advance.

🍽️

Central Mess & Dining Schedule

Four nutritious meals served daily: Breakfast (07:30–09:30 AM), Lunch (12:00–02:00 PM), Evening Snacks (05:00–06:00 PM), and Dinner (07:30–09:45 PM). Food is strictly prohibited inside personal bedrooms to maintain optimal campus hygiene.

🔇

Mandatory Silence Hours

Quiet learning hours are strictly observed between 10:30 PM and 06:00 AM daily. Playing loudspeakers, shouting, or loud hallway gatherings during these hours attract disciplinary demerit points and suspension of exchange privileges.

🚫

Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy

LPU maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against any form of ragging, physical harassment, or intimidation, as mandated by the Supreme Court of India and UGC Regulations. Violators face immediate rustication and police FIR filing.

⚡

Electrical Safety & Prohibited Items

Personal laptops, mobile phone chargers, and desk study lamps are permitted. High-wattage heating coils, electric kettles, immersion rods, and induction cooktops are strictly banned to prevent fire hazards and breaker trips.

🧳

Room Shifting & Inventory Protocol

Approved transfer passes must be executed within 72 hours. Students must surrender physical keys to their current Block Caretaker after a joint inventory inspection (bed, mattress, study desk, almirah) before new keys are issued.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you need to know about participating in the LPU room exchange program, multi-hop rotations, approvals, and fees.

❓ Who is eligible for hostel room exchange at Lovely Professional University? ▾

Any registered, bona fide LPU student currently residing in an official university hostel (Boys Hostels BH-1 to BH-10 or Girls Hostels GH-1 to GH-10) who has an active bed allotment, has cleared all semester hostel fee dues, and has no active disciplinary demerits or pending Proctorial Board inquiries is fully eligible to participate.

❓ Can male students exchange rooms with female students or between BH and GH? ▾

No, under no circumstances. Strict gender segregation is an immutable regulatory mandate at Lovely Professional University. Male students can only exchange rooms within Boys Hostels (BH-1 to BH-10), and female students strictly within Girls Hostels (GH-1 to GH-10). The portal's database engine enforces this constraint algorithmically at query time.

❓ How does the cyclic multi-hop (3-way or 4-way) swap engine work? ▾

In traditional manual systems, you can only swap if you find someone who wants your exact room and whose room you want in return. Our portal's Deterministic Cycle Discovery Engine connects multi-hop chains (e.g., Student A moves to B's room, Student B moves to C's room, and Student C moves to A's room). When a cycle closes, the system locks all affected beds simultaneously, verifies mutual consent, and presents a complete unified rotation.

❓ How are fee differentials handled when switching between AC, Non-AC, or different seaters? ▾

When swapping between different room categories (e.g., from a 3-Seater Non-AC to a 1-Seater AC), any pro-rated tariff differential is automatically computed by the University Accounts Division. The differential amount is directly credited or debited to your central University Fee Ledger (UMS account) upon final Caretaker inspection and digital transfer completion.

❓ What happens if one student in a proposed cycle rejects the proposal? ▾

All participants in a discovered exchange cycle must provide unanimous consensus. If any student declines the proposed room, the proposal status automatically transitions to 'DECLINED', the beds are unlocked immediately, and all other students are safely returned to the active queue to await alternate matching opportunities without penalty.

❓ How long does the Warden and DSW digital approval process take? ▾

Once all students in a rotation accept the swap, digital review alerts are transmitted to the respective Hostel Wardens. Reviews are conducted during daily office hours (typically within 24 to 48 business hours). Wardens verify academic standing, discipline records, and room compatibility before issuing the digital transfer pass.

❓ What is the procedure for physical key handover with the Block Caretaker? ▾

Upon final approval, you can download your official LPU Digital Transfer Pass containing a secure verification QR code. Bring this pass to your Block Caretaker desk. The Caretaker conducts a quick physical room inventory check (furniture, fittings, electricals), accepts your old keys, updates the master allotment ledger, and issues your new room keys.

❓ How can students with medical or physical accessibility needs request priority relocation? ▾

Students with verified medical conditions, mobility impairments, or elevator requirements can submit a Medical Priority Request. Submit your medical certificate endorsed by the Chief Medical Officer at the LPU Uni-Hospital to the Division of Student Welfare (DSW, Sector 34). Priority allotments are expedited outside the standard cycle queue.

Still have questions or need personalized guidance? Our DSW Hostel Support Desk is available Monday to Saturday, 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM.
